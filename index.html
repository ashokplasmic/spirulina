WHY THIS PROJECT

Wastewater can be
a starting point.

Educational institutions generate nutrient-rich wastewater. Conventional treatment can be costly, and useful nutrients may go unrecovered. This project explores a biological route that treats water while creating biomass for further use.

THE OPPORTUNITYConnect water treatment, resource recovery, energy, and biomedical research in one system.

THE MONITORING LAYER

A small board, a clearer picture.

The ESP32 gathers readings from the project’s temperature, humidity, and pH sensors. The dashboard presents those conditions together so the team can observe cultivation and document changes over time. Sensor readings support observation; they do not replace lab analysis or calibration.

WHAT MAKES THE APPROACH INTEGRATED

The presentation identifies a gap between separate algae cultivation, wastewater treatment, and biofuel workflows. The proposed innovation connects those areas with IoT observation and biomedical product exploration in one circular biorefinery concept.
